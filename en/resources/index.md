In 2010, on a huge government statistics project, my hands-on journey began on this path. Voice calls on mobile and landline — crores of minutes — and SMS interconnection wholesale pricing. Abundant documentation and some training taught me the power of data. Alongside that, since childhood I have believed in official documentation — what we call RTFM, 'Read The Fine Manual', going back to the newsgroup era. The GW-BASIC manual that came with my Commodore in childhood showed me how extraordinary official documentation can be. Hence my habit of reading the [scikit-learn](https://scikit-learn.org/) documentation. After all — the manual written by the person who built the product will be good; whose else could it be?

## Which book to use after which?

```bash
(Machine learning / NLP books)
1. ├── Red book = Hatekolome Machine Learning (2nd Edition)
-. | or
2. ├── Black book = out of print; existed as the 1st edition of the red book
3.     ├── White-yellow book = Python Machine Learning from Zero
4.         ├── Green-yellow = Hatekolome Python Deep Learning       # for advanced users
5.            └── Blue-white = Hatekolome Bangla Natural Language Processing   # for those who want to work with language
(Data analytics book)
[Hatekolome Data Analytics and Visualization]
7. Online now; print coming later
(Non-technical books, for everyone)
8. ├── In Search of Excellence: Bangladesh; A Humanistic State through AI
```
