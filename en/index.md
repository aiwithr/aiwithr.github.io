<div class="tile">
  <h2 class="post-title">Data, A Strategic Asset</h2>
  <p class="post-excerpt">Bangladesh is a land of boundless potential. To manage our massive workforce and its resources effectively, we must step into process optimization. And the first step is transitioning from manual processes to automation. That requires extracting knowledge from data. Hidden deep within data lies a wealth of untapped knowledge. We have the data; what we need is the knowledge to process it.</p>
</div><!-- /.tile -->

<div class="tile">
  <h2 class="post-title">Intelligence, For The System</h2>
  <p class="post-excerpt">To draw wisdom from data, one first needs a robust 'decision support system'. Even when humans remain in the decision loop, making sound decisions requires the mindset of extracting knowledge from data. An 'intelligent machine' accelerates that process, eliminating inconsistencies and friction across society. At the outset, start with rule-based systems. Across everything lies the decision process-flow.</p>
</div><!-- /.tile -->

<div class="tile">
  <h2 class="post-title"><a href="https://aiwithr.github.io/aibook/">Artificial Intelligence</a></h2>
  <p class="post-excerpt">Naturally, we want to design machines capable of thinking the way we do. We see this manifested in disease diagnosis, self-driving cars, airport management, long-haul flights, and beyond — meaning we want machines to stand beside us as collaborative partners, empowering us to make better decisions backed by data.</p>
</div><!-- /.tile -->

<div class="tile">
  <h2 class="post-title">Transferring Our Thinking to Machines</h2>
  <p class="post-excerpt">Much of how we humans think has already been imparted to machines. Whether an elevator accepts calls up to a specific threshold, or bypasses floor calls when overloaded — such intelligent routines are already active all around us. This transformation must expand everywhere, and it all begins with 'machine learning'. So, are you ready to learn?</p>
